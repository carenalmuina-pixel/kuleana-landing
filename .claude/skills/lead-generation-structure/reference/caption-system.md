# Caption / Creative System

A small, fixed design and copy system, applied consistently, so that every
single asset is instantly recognizable and reads as one coherent business —
consistency is itself a trust signal, independent of how good any individual
post is.

## Design system (fix these once)

- **Palette and type.** Pick a small, deliberate set (2-3 colors, 2-3
  typefaces) and never deviate — a feed that looks different every post
  reads as an unstable or disorganized business, even when the underlying
  work is excellent.
- **Format sizes.** Standard dimensions per placement (e.g., a 4:5 feed tile,
  a 9:16 reel/story) so nothing gets awkwardly cropped by the platform.
- **Legibility rules.** Decide once: no text directly over photography
  (use a framed image with copy on a solid background, or a solid scrim
  band under any text-over-photo treatment), sufficient contrast on every
  text style, no overlapping elements. Write the rule down so it survives
  past whoever originates the design system.

## The signature block

Every piece of content carries the same fixed block, so it becomes
structurally impossible to forget the pieces that matter most:

- The primary contact channel (phone, booking link, or the DM keyword — see
  `reference/dm-automation.md`).
- A one-line tagline or differentiator.
- The credential/license line, if the business is regulated
  (`reference/compliance-framework.md`) — exactly as that checklist
  specifies.
- A small, fixed hashtag or keyword set (don't reinvent this per post).

Bake the block into whatever template or generator produces the content, so
skipping it takes deliberate effort rather than being the default.

## Disclosure lines

Any staged, recreated, stock, or AI-generated imagery gets a disclosure
line *in the caption*, every time — worded so it survives being cropped or
reposted elsewhere (an on-image stamp alone can be cropped off). Keep the
wording consistent so it reads as a standard practice, not a one-off
disclaimer bolted onto a single risky post.

## Voice

Write in the customer's internal monologue, not brand-speak. Lead with the
moment of realization or the felt problem ("a small bubble in the ceiling is
never just a bubble," not "Acme Restoration offers ceiling damage
assessments"). Company name and credentials belong in the signature block,
not the hook.

## Signature series

Maintain at least one repeatable, numbered format — a specific visual
template reused deliberately with new specifics each time (a recurring
"case file," a numbered FAQ series, a numbered tip series). This becomes
recognizable in its own right and gives the business an evergreen content
engine instead of reinventing the format from scratch every time — pair it
with the content-planning backlog (`reference/content-planning.md`) as a
reliable, low-effort slot to fill.
