the **Commercial Claims Show** and **Public Adjuster Show** channels plus The
Claims Game podcast. His content teaches adjusters, not homeowners: claim

down to homeowner level — nobody owns "claim education for Hawaiʻi
homeowners" in short-form. His formats worth adapting: case-story arcs

(pointing at an actual policy page), and myth-vs-fact hooks.

**PA firms generally** (from the earlier competitive scan in
`content-ideas.md`): damage education dominates; almost no one does statute
updates, fee transparency, or consistent branding — all three are open lanes
for KUL.

## 4 · Hawaiʻi statute grounding (2020–2026)

- **2020 — Public adjuster standards (SB 2876):** Hawaiʻi adopted
  NAIC-model contract, fee, and conduct rules — incl. the 72-hour
  policy-limits fee limitation. (Basis for topics 27–28; already
  cross-referenced in `compliance.md`.)
- **2023 — Lahaina wildfires:** $3B+ insured losses; the shock that drove
  everything after. (Topic 24)
- **2024 — HHRF reactivated** by Gov. Green amid carrier pullback and
  premium spikes. (Topic 22)
- **2025 — Act 296 (SB 1044):** Hurricane Relief Fund formally revived to
  write hurricane coverage where the private market fails; ~$171M reserve
  split across HPIA support, the condo loan program, and hurricane
  insurance. Companion condo-stabilization laws launched the loan program
  for aging condos. (Topics 21, 23, 29)
- **2026 — reform package in motion:** SB 2966 (stop premium billing on
  destroyed homes; ALE usable for temporary/mobile housing on-site) and
  SB 2962 (rebuild or repurchase elsewhere, including outside the fire
  zone). Verify enactment status before scripting. (Topics 25–26)

Sources: Hawaii DCCA Insurance Division release (cca.hawaii.gov/ins),

## 5 · Production format (how each topic becomes a reel)

The pipeline that produced today's reels: 4 branded scenes (hook → 2–3
points → CTA) + Mahalo end card, 14–15s, license line on every scene,
crossfades, silent (add trending audio low in-app). Photo scenes pull from
`img/`; new imagery on demand. Compliance rules from `compliance.md` apply
to every script: no outcome promises, no fee percentages, disclose
recreations in captions. For law-update reels (topics 21–30): cite the act
number on-screen, keep to "what the law says," and avoid advising specific
coverage decisions.
