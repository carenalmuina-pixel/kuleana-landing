## M06 · Two adjusters — `m06-two-adjusters.png`

Two adjusters can look at the same roof and write two very different
estimates. The one the insurance company sends is estimating on the
carrier's behalf. A public adjuster is licensed by the state to estimate
on yours. Make sure someone in the conversation works for you.

## M07 · Ceiling stain — `m07-ceiling-stain.png`

That ring on the ceiling is not décor — it's a deadline. The longer water

Photograph it the day you see it, and don't paint over it before it's
documented.

## M09 · Hail — `m09-hail.png`

Hail doesn't knock — it bruises. From the ground the roof looks fine;
up close the granules are gone and the mat is soft. After any hail event:
note the date, photograph the gutters and AC fins, and get the roof
inspected while the evidence is fresh.

## M10 · Mold — `m10-mold.png`

Mold follows water. Every time. Behind drywall it can establish in about
72 hours — so the two things that decide these claims are speed and
documentation of the cause. Report fast, dry fast, photograph everything.

## M11 · Roof look-up — `m11-roof-lookup.png`

The 60-second roof check you can do from the yard: lifted shingle edges,
granules in the gutters, flashing pulling away. See any of the three after
a storm? Photograph it that day.
