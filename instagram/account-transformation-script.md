# Account Transformation Script — BrazucaInCopa → [NEW BRAND]

A step-by-step script for converting an existing, on-standby account (and its
follower base) into a new brand, instead of starting from zero. Written for
BrazucaInCopa (TikTok + IG + YT Shorts), but works for any standby account.
Fill every [BRACKET] before executing. Nothing here should be run until the
new brand is decided.

---

## Phase 0 — Decide before touching anything

1. **[NEW BRAND]** name, handle, one-line promise, target audience.
2. **Audience-overlap test (honest, 5 min):** BrazucaInCopa followers came
   for World Cup content — Brazilian/South Florida football fans, evening
   scrollers. The transformation works best if the new brand serves an
   overlapping identity (e.g., Brazilian community life in South Florida,
   soccer culture year-round, Brazilian-American lifestyle). If the new brand
   has near-zero overlap (e.g., B2B insurance), a fresh account will
   outperform a confused rebrand — in that case stop here and start clean.
3. **Keep or split platforms:** the TikTok, IG, and YT handles can transform
   independently. Transform only where the audience fits; leave the rest
   dormant.

## Phase 1 — Audit & preserve (Day 0)

- Export account data (IG: Settings → Accounts Center → Download information;
  TikTok: Settings → Download your data; YT: Google Takeout).
- Screenshot analytics: follower count, demographics (age/city/active hours),
  top 10 posts by reach and by saves. The active-hours chart determines the
  new posting windows — audience habits survive rebrands; content doesn't.
- Save all posted videos locally (the Content Master sheet already links
  sources).
- Decide per pillar: **Archive** old posts that fight the new positioning
  (archive, don't delete — history preserves account age and standing);
  **Keep** anything that still fits [NEW BRAND].

## Phase 2 — Identity swap (Day 1, all in one sitting)

Do these within one hour so the account never sits half-branded:

1. Handle → **[@newhandle]** (IG allows this instantly; the old handle is
   held ~14 days; TikTok allows one change per 30 days).
2. Display name → **[NEW BRAND]** — put the main search keyword in the name
   field, not just the brand word.
3. Profile photo → new mark. Bio → new one-liner + CTA link.
4. Wipe/rebuild Highlights to the new pillars; unpin old pins.
5. Post the **announcement reel** (Phase 3) and pin it.

## Phase 3 — The announcement reel (post at the audience's peak hour)

Script template, 20–30 seconds, face-to-camera (the face is the bridge —
it's the one thing followers recognize):

> **HOOK (0–3s):** "You followed me for [OLD THING]. Here's what's next."
> **BRIDGE (3–12s):** "[OLD THING] was a season — and what I loved most
> about it was [HONEST THREAD: the community / the stories / the culture].
> That's not ending. It's growing up."
> **REVEAL (12–22s):** "This account is now **[NEW BRAND]** — [one-line
> promise]. Same [voice/energy/face], new mission: [what they'll get,
> concretely, every week]."
> **CTA (22–30s):** "If you're here for [NEW VALUE], stay — [first thing]
> drops [day]. If not, no hard feelings — mahalo/obrigada for the ride."

Caption: the same message in two sentences + the new pillars as 3 bullets +
new hashtag set. Reply to every comment in the first 24 hours.

## Phase 4 — The 30-day bridge (build from there)

- **Cadence ramp:** 3×/week for weeks 1–2 (don't flood a repositioned
  audience), then to the new brand's full cadence.
- **Rule of thirds for the first 12 posts:** ⅓ familiar-format content in
  the new topic (keep the editing style/voice followers know), ⅓ pure
  new-brand value posts, ⅓ identity/story posts (why the pivot, who it's
  for).
- Re-cut the best-performing old formats with new-topic content first —
  proven hooks transfer better than new experiments.
- Expect and accept a follower dip (typically 5–15%); watch saves and
  DMs, not follower count, for the first month.

## Phase 5 — Measure & prune (Day 30)

- Compare reach/saves vs. the Phase 1 screenshots.
- Keep the two best new formats, kill the worst, lock the content calendar
  into the master queue (`schedule.md`) alongside MCC and KUL.
