# Kuleana Claim Solutions — Instagram Redesign (@yenclaims)

Redesign of the recurring post formats on @yenclaims, moved from Monarch Claims
Consultants branding (crown, #ClaimWhatsYours, Miami contact info) to the
Kuleana Claim Solutions brand system used on kuleanaclaims.com.

**Design system**

- **Palette:** terracotta `#B8501F` · deep green `#3F5A41` · gold `#F7DBA7` · cream `#FAF5EA` · ink `#2A2620`
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display), Cinzel (wordmark), Lora (body/eyebrows), Allura (one-word script accents)
- **Header:** every tile and video opens with the KULEANA name — K triquetra mark + Cinzel wordmark lockup — under the gold hairline
- **Compliance footer (every asset):** `HI Public Adjuster License #19246735 · Hawaiʻi DCCA Insurance Division · No recovery, no fee` — see `compliance.md` for the statute cross-reference
- **Format:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5) feed tiles · 720 × 1280 (9:16) reels
- **Legibility rules:** no text over photography (photo tiles use a framed image with copy on solid cream; reels put copy on solid scrim bands), no overlapping elements, WCAG-level contrast on every text style

Source files: `kuleana-instagram-posts.html` (self-contained — fonts and logo
embedded; open in a browser to view or re-screenshot), rendered tiles in
`posts/`, reels in `video/` (raw clips + brand frames included for re-edits).

**Imagery disclosure:** the damage photos and video clips are AI-generated
illustrative recreations, stamped "Illustrative recreation" on the artwork.
Keep that stamp. If you ever swap in a real claim photo, get written client
permission and crop out any claim numbers or addresses.
