# Avatar Reel Scripts — Batch 1 (Caren's voiceover)

Six scripts, ~70 words / 20–30 seconds each. **How to record:** phone voice
memo, quiet room, phone about a hand's width from your mouth, conversational
pace — like explaining to a neighbor, not reading an ad. One take is fine;
two takes are better. Send the memos here or drop them in Drive.

Numbers and spellings are written out the way they should be *spoken*.
On-screen cards, scenes, and captions are production notes — you only
record the **SCRIPT** block.

---

## Production checklist (per reel, after the memos arrive)

1. Clean the audio (enhance speech), trim to 20–30s.
2. Generate 2–3 Selenite scenes (2K stills → Kling 5s, 9:16).
3. Render brand text cards (HTML → transparent PNG), overlay, crossfade.
4. End card: license + (MCC) solicitation disclaimer on-screen.
5. Export 1080×1920 → Caren reviews → only then scheduled.

⚖️ = lead-gen: FL solicitation disclaimer required on-screen + in caption.
Keyword CTAs stay "DM me the word POLICY" until the automation in
`dm-automation.md` is live.
