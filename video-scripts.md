# Kuleana Claim Solutions — Video Scripts

Three ready-to-record scripts for client-facing example videos. Each is written
to be spoken naturally on camera (talking-head style, phone or DSLR), runs about
60–90 seconds, and ends with the same call to action so the series feels
consistent.

**General recording notes**

- **Tone:** Warm, steady, plain-English. You're a neighbor who happens to be a
  licensed expert — never a salesperson.
- **Setting:** Natural light, quiet background. A lanai, office with the Kuleana
  logo, or on-site at an inspection (hard hat / clipboard) all work.
- **Wardrobe:** Aloha-professional. Consistent across all three videos.
- **Pacing:** Speak slower than feels natural. Pause one beat at every
  paragraph break below.
- **Lower-third text:** "Caren Almuina · Licensed Public Adjuster · HI DCCA
  #19246735" on screen for the first 5 seconds of every video.
- **End card (all videos):** Kuleana logo + "(808) 444-9420 ·
  kuleanaclaims.com · No recovery, no fee."

## Video 1 — "Your insurance company has an adjuster. So should you."

**Purpose:** Introduce yourself and explain what a public adjuster is.
**Length:** ~75 seconds
**Suggested opening shot:** You walking toward camera outside a Hawaiʻi home,
or seated, looking straight into the lens.

### Script

> Aloha — I'm Caren Almuina, a licensed public adjuster here in Hawaiʻi, and I
> want to tell you something most homeowners only learn the hard way.
>
> When your home is damaged and you file a claim, your insurance company sends
> an adjuster. That adjuster is professional, polite — and works for the
> carrier. Their job is to manage what the company pays out.
>
> A public adjuster is the other side of that equation. I'm licensed by the
> same State of Hawaiʻi agency, but I'm hired by *you* — and I represent only
> your interests. I read your policy line by line, document every part of the
> loss, build the estimate myself in the same software carriers use, and
> negotiate until every covered dollar is honored.
>
> Your insurance company has an adjuster on their side. You deserve one on
> yours.
>
> The name of my firm is Kuleana Claim Solutions. Kuleana is a Hawaiian word
> with no clean English translation — it means both responsibility and
> privilege. That's exactly how I treat your claim.
>
> If your home has been damaged — or you just want to know what your policy
> actually covers — the first review is free. No recovery, no fee. Call me at
> (808) 444-9420. Your claim is our kuleana.

## Video 2 — "Four steps. One kuleana." (How the process works)

**Purpose:** Walk clients through exactly what happens when they hire you, and
remove the fear of cost.
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Suggested visuals:** Start seated at a desk with a policy in hand. If
possible, cut b-roll for each step: reading a declarations page, measuring or
photographing damage, on the phone, handing over/receiving a check.

### Script

> Aloha, Caren Almuina here with Kuleana Claim Solutions. Homeowners ask me
> all the time: "If I call you, what actually happens next?" It's four steps —
> and the first one costs you nothing.
>
> **Step one: the free policy review.** You send me your declarations page and
> anything the carrier has sent you. I read the exclusions and endorsements
> line by line and translate the legalese into plain English — so you know
> what you're actually owed. No charge, no obligation.
>
> **Step two: documentation.** I come to your home and measure the property
> myself. Photos, moisture readings, every affected area. Then I build the
> full estimate in Xactimate — the same line-item software the carriers use —
> so they're looking at a record they can't easily dispute.
>
> **Step three: negotiation.** I submit, defend, and re-submit that file until
> every covered loss is honored. You handle your home. I handle the carrier.
>
> **Step four: recovery.** The funds flow to you — not to me. I'm paid only a
> small percentage of what I actually recover, agreed in writing before you
> sign anything. If there's no recovery, there's no fee. Ever.
>
> That's it. No up-front cost, no pressure — just someone in your corner who
> does this every day. Call (808) 444-9420 and let's start with that free
> review.

| Step three | *3 · Carrier Negotiation* |
| Step four | *4 · Recovery — no recovery, no fee* |

## Video 3 — "A denial is a position, not a verdict." (Denied or underpaid claims)

**Purpose:** Reach the homeowner who already got a low offer or a denial and
believes it's over. This is your strongest emotional and proof-driven video.
**Length:** ~80 seconds
**Suggested visuals:** More serious, direct-to-camera framing. Show the
before/after numbers as bold on-screen graphics when you say them.

### Script

> If your insurance claim was denied — or the check they sent doesn't come
> close to covering the damage — please hear this before you give up:
>
> A denial is a position. It is not a verdict.
>
> I'm Caren Almuina, a licensed public adjuster with Kuleana Claim Solutions,
> and reopening claims like yours is a big part of what I do. With proper
> documentation — photos, moisture readings, a written narrative tied to your
> policy's actual language — most denied and underpaid claims can be
> challenged and recovered.
>
> Here's what that looks like in real numbers. One homeowner came to me with a
> settlement offer of four thousand nine hundred dollars. After we re-measured,
> re-documented, and rebuilt the estimate line by line, that claim resolved at
> over twenty-four thousand. Another family's claim had been denied outright —
> denied — and we brought it back to a forty-two-thousand-dollar recovery.
>
> You have the right to a second opinion, at any point in your claim — even
> after a denial. And it costs you nothing to find out where you stand: the
> review is free, and if there's no recovery, there's no fee.
>
> Don't let their first answer be the final answer. Call me at (808) 444-9420.
> Your claim is our kuleana.

## Optional variations

- **Spanish versions:** The website already carries full Spanish copy — these
  scripts translate cleanly if you want to record "Su reclamo es nuestro
  Kuleana" versions for Spanish-speaking clients.
- **Short cuts:** Each script's opening two paragraphs plus the closing CTA
  works as a 30-second cut for Instagram Reels / TikTok.
- **Recording order tip:** Record Video 2 first (most structured), then 1,
  then 3 — by the third you'll be warmed up for the most emotional delivery.
